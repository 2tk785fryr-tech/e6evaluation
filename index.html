Before you type anything

  • Nothing classified, NNPI, or CUI. No plant parameters, no design data, no casualty response procedures, no operational schedules.
  • Keep technical detail generic. "Propulsion plant trainer," "casualty control drill set," "watch station qualification." Don't describe what the drill actually was.
  • Counts are fine. Students trained, boards conducted, quals signed, hours delivered. Plant conditions are not.
  • Use your own device. Then hand-type the finished narrative into NAVFIT98A.
  • If you can't describe something without crossing a line, write that bullet yourself.

ITG bullet skeletons

Short Title Case lead-in, a period, then normal sentences. Fill in your own numbers.

Opening statement

Petty Officer [Name] is the instructor this division routes its hardest qualification cases to, and the watch section leader who returned [N] students to their crews on schedule.

Bullet 1 — leadership or mentoring (mandatory first)

- Develops Sailors. Mentored [N] junior personnel; [N] advanced in rate, [N] completed watch qualification, and [N] earned [recognition]. - Led From the Front. Supervised a [N]-person watch section through [N] evolutions with zero procedural compliance discrepancies.

Bullet 2 — technical knowledge

- Recognized Expert. Sought out site-wide for [system area] knowledge; resolved [N] technical questions and corrected [N] procedural errors before they reached students. - Mastered the Material. Scored [N]% on level-of-knowledge examinations, [N] points above the division average.

Bullet 3 — strongest remaining material

- Superior Watchstander. Stood [N] watches as [station] across [N] evolutions, generating [N] hours of trainer availability for [N] student crews. - Master Instructor. Delivered [N] hours of instruction to [N] personnel across [N] events; student exam average of [N]% exceeded the site standard. - Pipeline Enabler. Conducted [N] oral checkouts for newly reported students in support of OCTG, returning [N] qualified watchstanders to their crews on schedule. - Fixed the Process. Identified and corrected [problem], saving [N] man-hours per [period].

Closing statements

Petty Officer [Name] is a proven leader who excels at every task assigned, highly recommended for Chief Petty Officer. Petty Officer [Name] is an excellent leader, has my recommendation for advancement to Chief Petty Officer upon qualification as Engineering Watch Supervisor. Petty Officer [Name] is performing above paygrade and is ready now for assignment as [billet].

Boards read recommendations as a ladder — "highly recommended" sits above "has my recommendation", which sits above a plain "recommended". Claiming a rung your record does not support is more damaging than the honest one.

Verb bank

Use these instead of "was responsible for" or "assisted with."

ADVANCED · AUTHORED · BOARDED · BUILT · CERTIFIED · CONDUCTED · CORRECTED · DELIVERED · DEVELOPED · DIRECTED · DROVE · ELIMINATED · ENABLED · EXECUTED · IDENTIFIED · IMPLEMENTED · INSTRUCTED · LED · MANAGED · MENTORED · OVERHAULED · QUALIFIED · REDESIGNED · RESOLVED · REVISED · SPEARHEADED · STANDARDIZED · SUPERVISED · TRAINED · VALIDATED

How this goes wrong
  • Fabricated numbers. The fastest way to get a record questioned. If you don't know it, go find it. The prompt tells the AI to flag gaps instead of guessing — don't override that.
  • Duties instead of results. "Stood watch as EWS" is a job description. "Stood 47 watches as EWS, enabling 312 hours of trainer availability" is an eval bullet.
  • Everyone's eval reading the same. Differentiation comes from this worksheet, not the prompt. Thin inputs, generic output.
  • Leaving off the first half of the year. If you transferred in mid-cycle, section 08 is not optional for you.
  • Ignoring the soft traits. An all-technical narrative leaves Climate/EO and Military Bearing unsupported, and those marks won't defend.
  • Skipping the proofread. You sign it. You own it.
Using this on a phone or tablet
  • Save draft downloads a small file you can reopen later with Load draft. On iPhone it lands in Files or the share sheet; on Android it goes to Downloads.
  • Your work saves automatically on this device as you type, so you can close the tab and come back to it. Clearing your browser data wipes it — use Save draft for anything you want to keep for sure, or to move it to another device.
  • Print brag sheet opens your device's print dialog — pick a printer, or choose "Save as PDF" to email it to yourself.
  • If Copy doesn't work in your browser, tap into the prompt box, select all, and copy by hand.
